System Requirements

  • macOS 11 or later
  • Any valid IMAP/SMTP email account
  • Optional: Ollama running locally for AI triage (gaming PC or local GPU recommended)

Quick Setup

1. Install DarkMail

Download the DMG, drag DarkMail to Applications folder. Launch from Applications.

2. Add Your Email Account

In the DarkMail settings, enter your email address and IMAP/SMTP credentials. For Gmail, use an app-specific password.

3. (Optional) Enable AI Triage

If you have Ollama running on your network, point DarkMail to it in settings. Messages will be automatically categorized and scored.

4. Start Reading

DarkMail syncs your inbox every 5 minutes. Inbox stays up to date, spam auto-trashed, important floats to top.
