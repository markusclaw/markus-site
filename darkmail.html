The dial

You decide how much your bot handles

DarkMail ships with four control levels. Start conservative, dial up as you build trust. Change anytime.

Observer

AI reads and categorizes your mail. Never acts. You see triage scores but make every decision.

Read-only

Assistant

AI suggests actions and drafts replies. You approve before anything sends. Training wheels on.

Suggest + confirm

Co-pilot

AI auto-trashes spam, archives noise, and flags what matters. You handle the important stuff.

Shared control

Autopilot

AI manages your entire inbox. Responds, archives, escalates. You review a daily digest.

Full autonomy
What you get

Everything runs on your machine

No accounts to create. No data leaving your network. No subscriptions.

Full local control

IMAP/SMTP client running on your hardware. Your email never touches a third-party server. SQLite database you can query directly.

AI triage

Ollama scores every message for importance, categorizes it, and suggests what to do. Runs on your GPU — never phones home.

Dark native UI

Clean, minimal interface built for focus. Fast search, smart filtering, keyboard shortcuts, and distraction-free reading.

Any provider

Gmail, Outlook, Proton, Fastmail, Titan, or any IMAP provider. No vendor lock-in. No gatekeeping. Bring your own domain.

Standalone app

Ships as a macOS DMG. Drag to Applications, launch, done. No terminal needed. First-run setup wizard walks you through everything.

Open source

Full source on GitHub. Built with FastAPI + Python. Fork it, extend it, make it yours. Community-driven development.

Get started

Up and running in four steps

1

Install

Download the DMG, drag to Applications. Launch DarkMail — no terminal, no dependencies.

2

Connect your email

Enter your IMAP/SMTP credentials in the setup wizard. Works with any provider out of the box.

3

Set your bot level

Choose how much control to give your AI — from observer to full autopilot. Change it anytime.

4

Start reading

DarkMail syncs every 5 minutes. Spam gets auto-trashed, important floats to top. You own every byte.
